## About the project
Few owls have had their genome sequenced. Rice's mascot, Sammy, is a great horned owl (*Bubo virginianus*), and this project set out to sequence and assemble the genome of the species with Rice students doing the analysis.

The Houston Zoo provided a blood sample from a great horned owl, collected during a routine health exam. The Human Genome Sequencing Center at Baylor College of Medicine prepared the DNA and sequenced it with PacBio long reads, delivering the data in January 2022. A team of Rice students then assembled a draft genome of roughly 1.5 billion base pairs as a semester-long capstone project in Rice's Data to Knowledge (D2K) Lab, mentored by Todd Treangen and Huw Ogilvie.
