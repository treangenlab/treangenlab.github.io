## Background
Parsnp is a command-line tool for efficient microbial core-genome alignment and SNP detection. It is part of the Harvest suite, alongside the Gingr viewer for genome alignments and phylogenetic trees {% cite treangen2014harvest %}.

Parsnp v2 improves scalability and accuracy for large collections of genomes {% cite kille2024parsnp %}. Newer lab tools build on it, including [Strainify](/projects/strainify/).

## Availability
Open source at [github.com/marbl/parsnp](https://github.com/marbl/parsnp) and installable from Bioconda.
