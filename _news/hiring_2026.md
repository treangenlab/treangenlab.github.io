The Treangen Lab at Rice University has three open positions:

- **PhD student**
- **Postdoctoral Scientist**
- **Research Engineer**

We develop scalable algorithms, methods and open-source software for microbial genomics, with a focus on biosecurity, pathogen detection and microbiome research. You can read about the lab's current work on the [projects](/projects/) page and meet the group on the [people](/people/) page, where the three open spots are waiting.

To learn more or to apply, contact Todd Treangen at [treangen@rice.edu](mailto:treangen@rice.edu) with your CV and a short note about your interests and which position you are interested in.
