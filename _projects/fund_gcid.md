## About the project
The Texas Medical Center Genomic Center for Infectious Diseases (TMC GCID) was established by the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases in 2019 and renewed in 2024. It brings together basic and physician scientists at Baylor College of Medicine, the University of Texas-Houston School of Public Health, Houston Methodist Research Institute, MD Anderson Cancer Center and Rice University to profile hosts and microbes in infection models, with the aim of revealing pathogen genetic variants and host responses that can inform precision therapies.

The Treangen lab contributes computational methods for detecting and tracking genomic variation in pathogens, from viral intrahost variants to bacterial strains within a microbiome.

## Tools from this project
- [bronko](/projects/bronko/): alignment-free detection of viral genome variation
- [mft-tools](/projects/mft/): a sequence transformation for more sensitive viral genome alignment
- [Strainify](/projects/strainify/): strain-level profiling of low-coverage short-read metagenomes

Listed tools are those whose papers acknowledge this award in NIH RePORTER. Source code for all lab tools is on the [Code](/repositories/) page.
