## Background
KOMB combs through large metagenomes to find regions of biological interest, functional or taxonomic. It uses K-core decomposition of metagenome graphs to characterize copy number variation in microbiomes de novo, without relying on reference genomes {% cite balaji2022komb %}.

## Availability
Open source at [github.com/treangenlab/komb](https://github.com/treangenlab/komb).
