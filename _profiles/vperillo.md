Vanesa worked with the Treangen Lab on microbial mat communities, as part of the lab's [NSF MIM project](/projects/fund_mim/). Vanesa is now an Adjunct Researcher at CONICET (Consejo Nacional de Investigaciones Científicas y Técnicas) at Universidad Nacional del Sur in Argentina. She is lead author of the lab's survey of computational approaches for characterizing microbial interactions in microbial mats {% cite perillo2025survey %}.
