## Background
SeqScreen (Sequence of Interest Screening) characterizes short DNA sequences by the function of what they encode, not only by the organism they resemble. It combines alignment-based and machine learning annotation to report taxonomy, gene function and functions of sequences of concern tied to pathogenesis, which makes it useful for synthetic DNA screening and for finding pathogen signals in metagenomic data {% cite balaji2022seqscreen %}.

SeqScreen runs in fast, sensitive and Oxford Nanopore modes. SeqScreen-Nano extends the approach to long reads {% cite balaji2023seqscreen %}, and both build on the original SeqScreen biocuration platform {% cite albin2019seqscreen %}.
