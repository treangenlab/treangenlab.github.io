Michael (PhD, Bioengineering, Rice University; now a postdoctoral scientist in the Stadler Lab at Rice) obtained a B.E. degree in Biomedical Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2019. During his undergraduate studies, Michael did research in developing multiplexed droplet digital PCR (ddPCR) assays with Dr. Yong Guo. Before joining the Treangen Lab in 2022, he worked on design and optimization of multiplexed NGS assays with Dr. David Y. Zhang {% cite xie2022designing wu2022ensemble zhang2021deep %}. Michael's primary research interest is algorithms for multiplexed primer and probe design, under the setting of wastewater pathogen surveillance {% cite wang2024olivar wang2026seqwin wu2024multiplexed wolken2024prek %}. He likes playing all kinds of sports and loves music. 

In the Treangen Lab, Michael was supported by CDC award (75D30122C14709) and NSF CAREER (IIS 2239114).
