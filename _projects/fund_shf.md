## About the project
Analyzing unstructured data streams in real time is increasingly limited by energy use, both on small battery-powered devices and in data centers. This project designs a computing platform for detecting rich patterns in data streams. Patterns are written in formal specification languages based on extended regular expressions, translated into a new kind of automata, and matched on hardware co-designed for the task. Applications include network security, bioinformatics and data mining.

The project is led by Dr. Kaiyuan Yang with Dr. Konstantinos Mamouras at Rice. The Treangen lab brings the bioinformatics application: detecting pathogens directly from raw nanopore signal on small devices.
