Todd formed the Treangen Lab in July 2018. Given the computational challenges presented by the metagenomic data deluge, coupled with the time-sensitive nature of problems specific to tracking pandemics and synthetic DNA screening, the Treangen lab strives to develop efficient and accurate computational solutions to emerging problems in these fields. Specifically, his research group focuses on the design, development, and implementation of novel algorithms, heuristics, and data structures to solve emerging computational research questions specific to biosecurity, infectious disease monitoring, and host-associated microbiome characterization. The Treangen lab is also dedicated to the dissemination and development of novel open-source bioinformatics methods, software, and pipelines, and to providing “hands on” research opportunities to Rice undergraduates.

Todd leads the [AI for Biosecurity and Microbial Monitoring](https://kenkennedy.rice.edu/cluster-aimm) cluster at Rice's [Ken Kennedy Institute](https://kenkennedy.rice.edu).

## Events and awards

**DHS "Ready, Set... ID the Biothreat" prize challenge (November 2026).** Todd leads the Rice team, with Dr. Lauren Stadler and Dr. Yousif Shamoo, that was selected as one of eight Stage One finalists in the DHS Science and Technology Directorate's AI-assisted biodetection challenge. The challenge targets a problem that has long motivated the lab's research: identifying known and previously unrecognized biological threats in complex environmental data, without relying only on static lists of known pathogens. [Announcement](https://www.dhs.gov/science-and-technology/news/2026/11/17/dhs-st-announces-eight-finalists-stage-one-ready-set-id-biothreat-prize-challenge) · [Challenge overview](https://www.dhs.gov/science-and-technology/ready-set-id-biothreat)

**DARPA Bio-Attribution Challenge (2026).** Todd led the Rice team that placed second in Round 1 and received the Highest Accuracy distinction in the final awards. Round 1 required screening 250 terabytes of environmental sequencing data within eight hours; the final round involved 600 to 800 terabytes within 24 hours. [Challenge page](https://www.darpa.mil/research/challenges/bio-attribution) · [Final awards](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2026/bio-attribution-challenge-round-2) · [Rice News](https://news.rice.edu/news/2026/rice-computer-scientists-race-ahead-pathogen-detection-darpa-challenge)

**Atlantic Council, "From anthrax to AI: Biological attribution then and now" (November 12, 2026).** Todd was a panelist on "AI and the next attribution challenge" at this event convened by the Bipartisan Commission on Biodefense, bringing his background in microbial forensics and computational biosecurity to the discussion of biological attribution in the AI era. [Event agenda](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/event/from-anthrax-to-ai-biological-attribution-then-and-now/) · [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dwZweSiP5TY)
