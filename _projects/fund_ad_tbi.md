## About the project
*Microbiota-targeted approaches to resolve dysbiosis-induced AD neuropathology following brain injury* is led by Dr. Sonia Villapol at Houston Methodist Research Institute. People living with Alzheimer's disease are at higher risk of falls and traumatic brain injury, and their recovery is often poor. The project studies the brain-gut-microbiota axis in mouse models and works toward precision microbiota therapies that protect against neurodegeneration after brain injury.

The Treangen lab provides the microbiome profiling for these studies, using full-length 16S rRNA and long-read sequencing to follow how the gut community shifts after injury and in response to probiotic and antibiotic treatment.
