## About the project
Dynamics of Colonization and Infection by Multidrug-Resistant Pathogens in Immunocompromised and Critically Ill Patients (DYNAMITE) is a multi-institution program funded by an $11 million award from the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases. It asks why some critically ill patients go on to develop multidrug-resistant infections, focusing on the microbial, clinical and antimicrobial-resistance factors behind three major pathogens: vancomycin-resistant enterococci, Enterobacterales producing extended-spectrum beta-lactamases or carbapenemases, and *Clostridioides difficile*.

The Treangen lab develops the metagenomic and genomic analysis methods the program uses to profile patient microbiomes, resolve strains, and follow how pathogens and their genomes change over time.

## Tools from this project
- [Emu](/projects/emu/): species-level profiling from full-length 16S nanopore reads
- [Lemur and MAGnet](/projects/lemur_magnet/): fast taxonomic profiling of long-read metagenomes
- [Strainify](/projects/strainify/): strain-level profiling of low-coverage short-read metagenomes
- [Kente](/projects/kente/): pangenome-graph detection of horizontal gene transfer
- [Rhea](/projects/rhea/): reference-free structural variant detection in microbiomes
- [Bakdrive](/projects/bakdrive/): finding the species that drive microbial community interactions
- [Seqwin](/projects/seqwin/): signature sequences in microbial genomes, for assay design
- [bronko](/projects/bronko/): alignment-free detection of viral genome variation
- [mft-tools](/projects/mft/): a sequence transformation for more sensitive viral genome alignment
- [Crykey](/projects/crykey/): cryptic SARS-CoV-2 mutations in wastewater
- [SeqScreen](/projects/seqscreen/): functional screening of pathogenic sequences
- [MethPhaser](/projects/methphaser/): methylation-based haplotype phasing of long reads

Listed tools are those whose papers acknowledge this award in NIH RePORTER. Source code for all lab tools is on the [Code](/repositories/) page.
