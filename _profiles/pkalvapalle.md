Prashant (now a postdoctoral scientist in the Khakhar Lab in the [Department of Biology](https://www.biology.colostate.edu/staff/) at Colorado State University) was a postdoc in the Treangen Lab from January 2025 through May 2026, where he was the main developer of [somatem](https://github.com/treangenlab/somatem), the lab's modular Nextflow toolkit for long-read microbiome analysis. He also collaborated with the lab on wastewater pathogen monitoring {% cite wu2024multiplexed wang2024olivar %}.
