Nicolae completed a PhD in Computer Science at Rice in the Treangen Lab and is now a postdoctoral associate in Luay Nakhleh's lab in the Rice Department of Computer Science. In the lab, Nicolae led work on [Lemur and MAGnet](/projects/lemur_magnet/), on [KombOver](https://doi.org/10.1142/9789811286421_0039), which scales the lab's [KOMB](/projects/komb/) approach for tracking microbiome dynamics to large study cohorts, and on [QuaID](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-38184-3), a tool that detects emerging SARS-CoV-2 variants of concern in wastewater up to three weeks earlier.

Nicolae also led one of the first studies to characterize low-frequency, within-host variants of SARS-CoV-2. Published in [Genome Research](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.268961.120), it contrasted the diversity of the virus within individual hosts and between hosts across 129 RNA-seq data sets and 6,928 consensus genomes, and showed how within-host variants can inform the design of diagnostic tests and help track transmission.

His new preprint proposes the spectrum of a graph sheaf Laplacian (GSL) as a measure of microbiome diversity that accounts for genome architecture: [check it out on bioRxiv](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.10.710879). More of his current work is on [his personal website](https://nsapoval.github.io/).
