## Background
Wastewater monitoring complements clinical testing for COVID-19 surveillance. Crykey identifies cryptic SARS-CoV-2 mutations in wastewater: mutations, and combinations of mutations, that are rare or entirely absent from existing genome databases {% cite liu2024crykey %}.

Crykey finds mutations that co-occur on the same reads in wastewater samples, supports fast queries of mutation combinations against all publicly available SARS-CoV-2 genomes, and helps study intrahost evolution and transmission at scale.
