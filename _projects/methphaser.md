## Background
MethPhaser uses the methylation signal in Oxford Nanopore reads to extend haplotype phasing of human genomes beyond what SNV-based phasing achieves alone {% cite fu2024methphaser %}. It works on autosomes and is recommended for read sets with at least 30X coverage.

## Availability
Open source at [github.com/treangenlab/methphaser](https://github.com/treangenlab/methphaser) and installable from Bioconda.
