## About the project
Houston's system for tracking COVID-19 through wastewater became the basis of an epidemiology center that the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention designated a National Wastewater Surveillance System Center of Excellence in 2022. The Houston Health Department leads the center in partnership with Houston Public Works and Rice University, and it supports research and innovation in wastewater surveillance nationwide.

The Treangen lab develops the genomic methods behind the sequencing side of this work: designing amplicon panels for target pathogens and detecting emerging and cryptic variants in mixed wastewater samples.

## Tools from this project
- [Olivar](/projects/olivar/): automated primer design for tiled amplicon sequencing
- [Olivia](/projects/olivia/): learned sequence representations for automated primer design
- [Crykey](/projects/crykey/): cryptic SARS-CoV-2 mutations in wastewater
- [QuaID](https://gitlab.com/treangenlab/quaid): early detection of emerging SARS-CoV-2 variants of concern in wastewater

Source code for all lab tools is on the [Code](/repositories/) page.
