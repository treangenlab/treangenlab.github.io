Welcome to the Treangen lab website, a bioinformatics and computational biology research group at Rice University, led by Dr. Todd J. Treangen (Associate Professor of Computer Science, with joint appointments in BioSciences and Bioengineering).

We're recruiting!

The Treangen Lab has three open positions: a PhD student, a Postdoctoral Scientist and a Research Engineer. Read about the open positions, or contact Todd Treangen at treangen@rice.edu.

Since launching in 2018, the Treangen lab has focused on developing scalable and accurate computational algorithms, methods, and open-source software tools to answer research questions specific to: (i) biosecurity and synthetic DNA screening (), (ii) pathogen genomics and wastewater surveillance (), (iii) microbiome profiling and metagenomics (), and (iv) scalable algorithms and AI for microbial genomics ().

Current funding includes NSF (CAREER, SHF, MIM), NIH (NIAID GCID), and DTRA, among others. Prior support from: c3.ai DTI, DHS, IARPA, and NIH.
