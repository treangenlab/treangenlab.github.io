## About the project
Reading and writing DNA is now routine and affordable, but the computational tools for tracking pathogens that have not been seen before, and for preventing intentional or unintentional misuse of synthetic DNA, have not kept pace. This NSF CAREER award supports new computational methods for pathogen detection and monitoring that go beyond matching sequences against fixed lists of known organisms. The methods are being brought together in a platform called GuarDNA for genomics-based biosecurity and biosurveillance.

## Tools from this project
- [Seqwin](/projects/seqwin/): signature sequences in microbial genomes, for assay design
- [Olivar](/projects/olivar/): automated primer design for tiled amplicon sequencing
- [Parsnp](/projects/parsnp/): core-genome alignment for thousands of microbial genomes
- [Rhea](/projects/rhea/): reference-free structural variant detection in microbiomes
- [KOMB](/projects/komb/): K-core characterization of copy number variation in microbiomes
- [somatem](/projects/somatem/): a modular Nextflow toolkit for long-read microbiome analysis (also supported by the Rice Department of Computer Science)

Source code for all lab tools is on the [Code](/repositories/) page.
