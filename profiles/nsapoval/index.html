Dr. Nicolae Sapoval

PhD, Computer Science. Now a postdoctoral associate in the Nakhleh Lab at Rice.

metagenomics machine learning viral genomics

Nicolae completed a PhD in Computer Science at Rice in the Treangen Lab and is now a postdoctoral associate in Luay Nakhleh’s lab in the Rice Department of Computer Science. In the lab, Nicolae led work on Lemur and MAGnet and on SARS-CoV-2 genomic analysis.
